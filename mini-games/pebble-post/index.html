Small packages.

  1. Make your rounds.

    Move the little courier with arrow keys, WASD, or the direction buttons. Push every orange parcel onto a dotted stamp.

  2. Think one step ahead.

    You can push one parcel at a time, but never pull. Watch the corners. Undo (Z) takes back any step, even after a delivery.

  3. Find the neatest route.

    Five handcrafted stops grow from a doorstep to a busy sorting yard. Par is the shortest possible route. Finish in fewer moves to improve your best.
