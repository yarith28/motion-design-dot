Crossgrain English word list

lexicon.mjs is derived from ENABLE (Enhanced North American Benchmark
LEexicon), compiled by M. Cooper and Alan Beale and released into the public
domain. The original declaration explicitly permits game designers to
incorporate the list into their games.

Public-domain declaration reproduced in LibreOffice's dictionary notices:
https://api.libreoffice.org/share/readme/LICENSE.html
Search that notice for "The ENABLE package".

Local transformation: alphabetic entries of length 2 through 9 only (the
board is 9 squares wide), uppercased, deduplicated and sorted. 105241 entries.
No definitions or proprietary dictionary text are included.

familiar-lexicon.mjs is the separate, locally curated opponent search
vocabulary. Every proposed play must still validate against lexicon.mjs.
