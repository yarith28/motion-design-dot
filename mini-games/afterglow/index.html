A field guide to afterglow

01 / Line it up. Drag on the field, move the aim slider, or use ← →. Press Release or Space. Walls are your friends.

02 / Make it bloom. Numbers are hits left. ✳︎ blooms burst into neighbors; + seeds add a firefly to your next volley.

03 / Keep your ground. Pods descend after each volley. Clear the garden before they touch the soil. A rescue pushes them back; run out and the expedition ends.

04 / Grow your way. Choose an upgrade after each garden. Clear all three to greet the dawn.
