  1. Make room.

    Tap a tile beside the empty space to slide it. Only horizontal and vertical neighbors can move.

  2. Bring it all together.

    Arrange 1–8 in reading order, with the empty space at bottom right. Each shuffle starts from the goal using legal moves, so it is always solvable.

    Arrow keys move the empty space. You can also Tab to a tile and press Enter / Space. Show goal gives you a reference without changing your puzzle.
