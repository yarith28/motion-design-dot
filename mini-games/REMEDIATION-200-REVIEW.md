# Small Hours — independent remediation review

Review date: 2026-10-01. Scope: improvements to the existing 200-game collection, following review of expansion commit `fb1aabe76e6e2420ac68dce1941ffe8d940c924c`.

## Verdict

The sampled remediations materially improve the collection. Safe Return and Convoy Ledger now require planning across time; Seed Tomorrow requires interventions between nonlinear growth steps; Ferry's changed compatibility networks require real rearrangement; Transit Observatory requires designing a whole identifying schedule. These are implemented decision differences, not just revised descriptions.

No unresolved blocker remains in the independently sampled remediations after the follow-up corrections below. Approval is qualified by the ordinary-play scope below and successful final source-matched regressions/publication checks. These games remain short studies with uneven depth. A successful automated route does not establish a satisfying opponent, every random seed's usability, or physical-phone/screen-reader accessibility. The collection should not be described as 200 equally deep games.

## Design changes challenged during review

- Insurance/escort redesigns were required to create future opportunity costs, not merely add another scenario with different numbers. Distinct contract durations, collateral release, lane trip lengths and irreversible losses meet that requirement in the sampled play. Exhaustive strategy counts and greedy comparisons reported by the implementers are supplementary test evidence, not independent ordinary-play wins.
- Seed Tomorrow was required to interleave limited interventions with nonlinear evolution; freely choosing a toggle subset at the beginning would remain too close to existing toggle puzzles. The current public behavior meets the stronger design.
- Scissor Lift was challenged against replacing the old universal height-1 solution with universal height 2. Different above/below-deck envelopes and two aisle geometries defeat that shortcut in an independently tested ordinary browser run.
- Foil was challenged against universal parry/strike or passive recovery loops. The tested contingent policy succeeds easily; the game now requires reading intent and stamina but should retain a modest-depth description.
- Discovery's shrinking-candidate family was required to change decision structure: batch schedule design, classifier construction from incomplete examples, fault repair, full-catalog decision trees and inverse reconstruction. Different subject matter alone was not accepted as sufficient differentiation.
- Archive's dependency-free later releases were rejected as insufficient support for the redesigned dependency-graph claim. A meaningful selected dependency must occur in each final generated release.
- Lens Library's initial redesign was rejected when visible source candidates permitted an arithmetic shortcut without calibration. The proposed correction uses editable reconstruction and genuinely ambiguous lens/source pairs; the follow-up independently certified a plate after two informative probes and manual reconstruction.

A separate independent reviewer also completed all three Clockmaker scenarios through ordinary UI inputs. Track Exchange initially failed this review: a perimeter bypass let the two-stage relay finish after only one transfer. After the topology correction, the old exploit failed, the held-signal bypass failed, and legitimate public-input paths won all three scenarios with one/two/two transfers respectively. The primary reviewer inspected the resulting action logs and terminal evidence; these are separate-reviewer executions, not additional primary-reviewer completions.

## Usability findings caught and corrected

1. Large tabletop control actions initially restored focus to tiny map controls. Repeated actual-input testing after the fix restored focus to large `Select …` controls instead.
2. Quiet Foil's final victory still said “NEXT BOUT READY” although there was no next bout. The owner removed that misleading final-state text.
3. Convoy's terminal return countdown was relative to an internal next wave despite the display remaining on wave 7/7. The owner hid the no-longer-relevant countdown after closing.

These corrections do not substitute for actual assistive-technology testing. Programmatic focus retention, readable DOM text and emulated tap targets are useful but limited evidence.

## Remaining verification limits

- Ordinary play is sampled as listed, not 100 unaided completions and not exhaustive alternate-state coverage.
- The primary reviewer completed only the default Systems scenarios; the second scenarios rely on separate implementation/regression evidence.
- No physical phone, actual screen reader or independently run local WebKit session is claimed.
- Direct live-site access was blocked by the review environment's network proxy. Before remediation, exact-commit CI logs independently corroborated live asset matching at 04:44 UTC; that evidence does not establish the later remediation's deployment. Final publication and final source-matched suites must be verified separately.
- The prior aggregate counted terminal losses as completion. The remediation requires winning tabletop main routes; final suite results should state wins separately from deliberate losses.
- Review was iterative. Early ordinary-play logs may precede later text, focus and generator corrections. Final source hashes identify the final reviewed snapshot, not an assertion that every historical log ran against identical bytes.
