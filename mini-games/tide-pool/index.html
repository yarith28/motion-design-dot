No clock. Just a little ocean.

Let the tide
bring it together.

  1. Choose your current.

    Your connected pool begins in the top-left corner. Choose a color below the board: all your glass changes to it.

  2. Gather the edges.

    Matching glass touching your pool above, below, left or right joins it. Think a few moves ahead to open new shores.

  3. Meet the horizon.

    Gather every tile before your moves run out. Clear all three pools to bank your score. Each unused move earns 100 points, each cleared pool 500.

Tap a color, Tab then Enter / Space, or use keys 1–5. Colors have distinct shapes. This is turn-based: leaving the tab never costs a move. Every fresh pool has a verified solution within its budget.
