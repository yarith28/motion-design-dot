  1. Follow the thread.

    Solve five clues. Tap letters in order to build each answer. Every tile is used exactly once.

  2. Find your own pace.

    Each word starts at 100 points. A hint reveals the next letter for 20 points; a wrong check costs 10. Words always earn at least 20. No timer.

  3. Type a little.

    Letter keys select tiles. Backspace removes the last letter; Enter checks the word or opens the next clue. Tab and Enter also operate every button.
