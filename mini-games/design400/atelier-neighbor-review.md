# Atelier source-neighbor inspection

Planning evidence only; this is not an implementation quality or browser-play claim.

Inspected inventory: `inventory.json` total300,300 unique listed games. Compared the20 atelier proposals with every catalog mechanic and the other80 design400 candidates.

Direct source checks:

- `systems-room/kiln-house.mjs`: two thermal masses with shell/core lag, fuel, dwell band and safe cooldown. Atelier interference assembly changes physical clearance and permanently locks assembly order; drawplate changes permanent plastic strain, section, length and hardening.
- `spatial-room/room.js`: Gear Train swaps adjacent tooth-count gears to match every static ratio. Clutch Dock mixes angular momentum between separate inertias and charges slip energy. Balance Mobile places fixed mass values on moment arms; Pulley Bay constrains continuous rope length and coupled moving-pulley strokes.
- `construct-room/engines.js`: Fair Portions guillotine-cuts selected polygons into target weighted masses/emblem counts. Common Cut extracts fixed silhouettes with kerf, nesting and clamp connectivity. Bonding Room joins contacting atoms into a new rigid graph; Interlock Cabinet instead preserves separate solids while clearances and3D legal slides determine disassembly.
- `discovery-room/room.js`: Wave Desk linearly sums independent basis amplitudes. Cam Workshop edits the neighboring radial profile, its contact slope and two phase-offset followers. Fossil Brush directly erases known cell depth using broad/precise tools; Crack Compass steers autonomous stress-based fracture, and Mill Fixture has3D tool accessibility and fixture support. Lens Library identifies a convolution kernel through probes and reconstructs pixels; Lens Craft combines refractive ray-height/ray-angle transforms and clips rays at apertures. Invisible Ink observes binary spectral evidence; proposed Polarization Stage manipulates ordered light states.
- `motion-room/navigation.js`: Threaded Return preserves rope winding history and rejects steps exceeding length. Capstan Crossing instead assigns friction wrap capacity and controlled release effort.
- `motion-room/physics.js`: After the Levee conserves water on a heightfield under soil transfer. Vented Cast tracks gas escape components that liquid closes, and Sand Table uses stable granular slope thresholds. Span Workshop solves linear axial truss reactions under a cart; Seal Shop distributes contact compression around a gasket.
- `word-room/room.js`: One Letter Away replaces one letter on one persistent ladder word. Sound Splice exchanges suffixes between two persistent phoneme ribbons; every accepted action changes both words and retains legal vocabulary on both sides.

Strongest cross-family risks before acceptance:

1. Polarization Stage / Signal Lab Phase Paper: ordered two-state phase transformations could become near-equivalent. A redesign or strong optical-specific demonstration is required before accepting implementation.
2. Capstan Crossing / Field Room Belay Line: both use rope friction. Atelier must require force-ratio route design and a hold-versus-release conflict, while Field Room owns dynamic slack, descent and shock loading.
3. Clutch Dock / Field Room Clutch Circuit: inertia synchronization/slip-energy must remain the defining atelier decision; gearbox selection/road racing belongs to Field Room.
4. Common Cut / Mill Fixture: Common Cut owns2D nesting, common kerf and released sheet pieces. Mill Fixture owns3D approach collision, cavity accessibility and re-clamping order. Dropping either distinction would make them weak neighbors.

No game accepted from this written review alone. The actual source transitions and meaningful ordinary-input scenarios must establish the distinctions.

Final implemented distinctions: Polarization Stage applies lossy Jones projections to two displayed incident beams and certifies transmitted intensity plus polarization ellipse; Phase Paper uses normalized coherent qubit operations and measurement. Capstan Crossing chooses a static holding-and-release wrap product with rope-stock constraints; Belay Line controls changing descent slack, brake friction and shock load. Clutch Dock synchronizes angular momentum and pays engagement/start resistance; Clutch Circuit selects road-racing gear ratios and manages engine/brake heat. Common Cut deletes 2D stock adjacencies to release nested silhouettes; Mill Fixture removes accessible 3D cavity voxels while retaining a clamped connected body. These distinctions are exercised by per-game coupled-rule regressions and native-control campaigns in the Atelier coverage report.
