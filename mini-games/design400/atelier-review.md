# Atelier mechanics and endless review

Inventory:20 new games. Every game preserves a finite three-study campaign. Ten games expose checked generated endless contracts; the remaining ten keep finite campaigns and record their generator prerequisites below. This review describes actual engine transitions, not a claim of browser test completion.

| Game | Defining state transition | Optional mode | Review / prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cam Workshop | Irreversible cam cuts change two phase-coupled neighboring-radius traces and cyclic contact slope. | Generated challenge stream | Seeded neighboring-safe radius mutations produce varied cyclic profiles and sampled dual-follower traces. Stock, fixed sectors and follower phase remain coupled. |

All modes remain turn-paced with native keyboard/touch controls. The finite campaign is never replaced by endless. Endless restarts at zero, has no point-farming reset/undo, ends on the first terminal failure, and permits explicit banking.

Verification lives in `tests/atelier-room.cjs`, `tests/atelier-room-plans.mjs`, `tests/atelier-room-regressions.mjs` and the generated source-bound `coverage/atelier-room.json`. Source-derived model paths and browser reachability are explicitly distinct from unaided human play; physical-device and screen-reader tests are outside that coverage.
