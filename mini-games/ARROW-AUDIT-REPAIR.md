# Arrow Audit generator repair

Final500-game regression found a real initialization hang in the preserved Arrow Audit game. The old generator chose one undirected graph outside its retry loop, then repeatedly changed the directions of exactly two edges at the first study. A path has no cycle under any orientation. A five-edge cycle also cannot become a directed cycle by reversing exactly two of its initially ordered edges. In either case the retry condition could never succeed, blocking the browser's main thread when Start was activated.

The [isolated original-source diagnostic](coverage/arrow-audit-original-generator-proof.json) reproduces the hang with constant0.99 and a deterministic random stream seeded20. [Native touch](coverage/arrow-audit-original-native-seed20.json) reaches the real Start handler and times out. A separate [natural-random observation](coverage/arrow-audit-original-natural-observation.json) returns every original random value unchanged and reproduces the hang on its31st native Start. These are deliberately failing diagnostics, not passing gameplay evidence. They bind to the original engine SHA256 `d6de5a4d7a8efe01682a384259c780a3fa88ecd75f32ffa5d9eaa46167d0ebcd`.

The repair changes only Arrow Audit's `init(stage)` in the shared Puzzle Lab engine. Every graph now contains a short triangle. Normal orientation attempts are capped at128; if they do not produce an acceptable cycle, a constructive fallback reverses the two identified path edges of that triangle. Restoring those edges gives an acyclic graph within two reversals. The original exact minimum-reversal calculation still determines the player's budget, including budgets of one, two or three. The remaining19 engines, render/check functions, controls, counters, three-study progression and preview bytes are unchanged.

The complete original module is archived at [preservation/historical/puzzle-lab-engines.js](preservation/historical/puzzle-lab-engines.js). Historical baseline fixtures and audit hashes retain their original values. The preservation checks explicitly identify this one repaired runtime file and require exact equality outside the initialization region; they reject broader edits or additional exceptions. Historical audit rows resolve to the checked archive, while the current500-game audit binds to the repaired source.

[The dedicated regression](tests/puzzle-arrow-audit-regression.cjs) independently checks cycle detection, minimum budgets and legal solutions across768 seed/stage cases and six constant-random cases, plus deterministic replay. Constant0.99 exercises the bounded fallback at all three stages. Four real keyboard/touch campaigns under the former failing streams complete twelve studies, reject the initial unsolved cycle, exercise Undo/Reset, verify edit/check counters, restart after winning and report zero page errors. The [actual regression report](coverage/puzzle-arrow-audit-regression.json) records source hashes, actions, witnesses and browser identity. Seed injection is test-only; this legacy game does not advertise a public seed-replay feature.

Full Puzzle Lab completion, catalog routes and generic controls are rerun after this repair. Exact-commit publication CI also runs this diagnostic regression against the deployed Chromium and WebKit interfaces. Browser touch is emulated; finite samples do not constitute an exhaustive random-sequence or physical-device proof.
