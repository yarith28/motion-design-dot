  1. One touch ripples outward.

    Tap a lantern to flip it and its immediate up, down, left and right neighbors. Glowing amber means on; a hollow ring means off.

  2. Find the dark.

    Turn every lantern off to finish a chapter. Three boards grow from a courtyard to a whole neighborhood. Every board is made with legal toggles and has a solution.

  3. Take your time.

    Use arrow keys to explore the board, then Enter or Space to toggle. Undo reverses your last move. Show a step marks one useful lantern; following these hints always leads home.
