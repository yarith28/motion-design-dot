Precision—

Turn over two cards. Find their other halves.

A little attention, doubled

Some things
come in pairs.

  1. Turn two.

    Flip any two cards. Matching shapes stay face up; mismatches turn back after a moment.

  2. Make a mental map.

    Find all eight pairs in as few turns as possible. There is no timer. Take your time.

  3. Tap, or use your keys.

    Tap cards, or use arrow keys to move focus and Enter / Space to flip. A second tap on the same card does not count.
