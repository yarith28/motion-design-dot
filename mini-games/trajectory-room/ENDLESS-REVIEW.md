# Trajectory Room optional endless review

This review follows the actual initializers, action rules, finite progression, completion/failure conditions and generator branches. A challenge stream starts a fresh seeded bounded challenge after each win, banks the score once and continues until a failure or the player's Bank and end run action. Each challenge restores its own finite physical reserves. All three-study finite campaigns remain available.

Generators select bounded families and physical ranges independently of unbounded stage indices; they do not increase a quota until it becomes impossible. The per-game source review and model suite distinguish 24 dispersed initializations, substantive physical-state variation (24 distinct sampled states for most games; 15 for the bounded discrete Refractory Route), and eight actual legal-control late-stage wins per supported game. These are sampled source/model properties, not a formal proof of every generated challenge.

Native optional evidence in `coverage/trajectory-room.json` separately records two actual generated-challenge wins per supported game/profile, fresh rendered content, cumulative scores, the player's end-run action, natural loss and restart. It does not label model-only late-stage witnesses as browser play. A current passed report with matching source hashes is required before release. Stance Circle should remain finite until an actual match generator and equivalent reachability/native evidence exist.
