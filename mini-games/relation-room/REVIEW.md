# Actual mechanics and optional-mode review

This source review covers exactly20 Relation games. All preserve three finite studies. Seventeen support seeded challenge streams or tactical match series with capped sizes; three retain honest finite-only curricula. Optional flags are conditional on the executed legal-witness and browser evidence, not merely a generator function existing.

No engine imports its verification planner. Runtime generators use constructive valid instances and discard the generating solution. Correctness checks accept alternative witnesses satisfying the actual relations; tests include nonedge preservation, endpoint contact, public-update effects, modal vacuity, quotient orientation, crossing bonds, leading zeros, fill edges, augmenting-path rerouting, ring eligibility, family transmission, positive posterior updates, min-heap inequalities, DAG reduction, quadrant rotation, quantifier order, global lie consistency and unreachable/outgoing invariant transitions.

Score is earned by completing a study or tactical duel. The shell banks a win once when advancing; Reset never banks a second copy of its score. Recall and paid Bayesian investigation disable Undo even in finite attempts. Optional modes disable Undo/Reset and preserve accumulated score when the player ends the run. Every supported game has late-stage legal witnesses; that evidence is model-only unless the browser report explicitly says otherwise.
